# Clip statement test

You will judge 300 short statements about short film clips: is each statement true or false, based only on the frames shown?
It takes about an hour, and you can take breaks.

## How to start

1. Download this repository (green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**) and unzip it, or clone it.
2. Open `index.html` in Firefox or Chrome. Keep the `frames/` folder next to it. Nothing needs to be installed.
3. Enter your name at the top and read the instructions (press `?` to see them again).

## What to do

- Each screen shows one statement and the frames of a clip (about 10 to 18 seconds, 2 frames per second, no sound). Play the clip or
  click through the frames as often as you like.
- Answer **true** (`1`) or **false** (`2`) for every statement, even when you are not sure: give your best guess.
- Tick **"I did not find this detail in the frames"** (`q`) when you looked but could not find what the statement is about, so your
  answer is a guess.
- Tick **"The statement is unclear"** (`w`) when you cannot tell what it claims.
- `Enter` goes to the next statement. Each clip appears twice, with different statements: judge each one on its own.
- Please do not look anything up, and do not discuss the statements with the other people doing the test until everyone is done.

## When you are done

Press **Export** and send the downloaded file (`p1_human_<name>_<date>.json`) to the person who asked you to do the test. Your answers are
saved in the browser while you work; to continue on another computer, Export there and Import the file here.

The frames come from film clips and are used here for research only.
