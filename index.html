P1 human test

How it works

You will see 300 short statements, one at a time, each with the frames of a short film clip (about 10 to 18 seconds, 2 frames per second, no sound). For each statement, decide whether it is true or false based only on these frames.

  • Answer every statement, even when you are not sure: give your best guess.
  • Some statements are true and some are false. Each clip appears twice, with different statements: judge each statement on its own.
  • People are described without names ("a man", "a girl"). Take the statement as it is written.
  • Play the clip or click through the frames as often as you like. There is no time limit, but please do not look anything up.

The two boxes (tick only when they apply; both can be ticked)

qI did not find this detail in the frames. You looked but could not find the thing the statement is about (too small, too short, or you could not spot it), so your answer is a guess.
wThe statement is unclear. You cannot tell what it claims: odd or wrong words, broken grammar, or more than one reading.

Saving and sending

Answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back. It takes about an hour in total; breaks are fine. When you are done, press Export and send the file (p1_human_<name>_<date>.json). To move to another computer, Export there and Import here.
